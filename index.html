The final chapter

Knights of the Round Table
The Metamorph Merlin

For more than a decade, the Knights of the Round Table have carried the Arthurian legend onto the dials of the Maison. The fourth book follows Merlin, wizard, prophet and counsellor to King Arthur. After his omniscience and his enchantments, this final chapter turns to the power he kept for the forest: the freedom to change form, and to walk Brocéliande as a white stag. The Metamorph Merlin, our last chapter, limited to 28 pieces.

  1. Book IV, Chapter I

    The Omniscient Merlin

    It is said that Merlin once used his magic to lift the hexagonal stones from the Giant’s Causeway and transport them to England, where he built an immortal monument that some believe to be Stonehenge.

    Edition complete

  2. Book IV, Chapter II

    The Enchanter Merlin

    Merlin’s devotion to the Lady of the Lake takes the shape of a hidden crystal palace playing with light and reflection, invisible to the world yet filled with enchantment.

  3. Book IV, Chapter III

    Merlin takes his final form

    In Brocéliande, Merlin passes through the trees as a white stag, between the visible and the invisible worlds. The Metamorph Merlin takes its green from that forest: a green CVD coating on glass and pink gold, from the base of the dial to the flange and the crown, set against the pink gold of the 45 mm case.

A forest of 57 blocks

The dial

Fifty-seven blocks, four materials, four decoration techniques. Shot-blasted and polished glass with green CVD coating, polished pink gold, transparent glass and Crystallium® inlays rise to different heights on the hexagonal language of the Giant’s Causeway, born in the first chapter.

Engraved for the first time

The case

For the first time in the collection, the pink gold case middle is engraved by hand, in micro-relief, around seventy hours of work for a single case. The white stag, symbol of metamorphosis, then the sage holding his book, a quiet nod to the first chapter. Sword-shaped guards frame the crown, itself inlaid with green CVD-coated pink gold, and a sapphire crystal ring runs under the bezel.
